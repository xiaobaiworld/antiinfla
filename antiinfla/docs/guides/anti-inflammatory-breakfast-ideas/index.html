Anti-Inflammatory Breakfast Ideas

A useful anti-inflammatory breakfast does not need to be complicated. The easiest breakfast patterns are the ones built from repeatable combinations of oats, berries, seeds, nuts, and simple drinks.

Breakfast patterns that work well

  • Oats with blueberries and chia seeds
  • Yogurt with strawberries and walnuts
  • A simple berry bowl with green tea or matcha
  • Savory breakfasts that add spinach and garlic when desired

How to use this guide

Choose one grain or base, one fruit, one seed or nut, and one drink. The goal is a breakfast pattern you can repeat, not a perfect recipe every day.
