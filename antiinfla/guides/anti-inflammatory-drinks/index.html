Anti-Inflammatory Drinks

The best anti-inflammatory drinks are usually the simplest ones you can use consistently. This guide focuses on tea-based habits and realistic daily routines instead of hype-driven beverage trends.

Simple drink ideas

  • Green tea with breakfast or an afternoon break
  • Matcha as a tea-based daily routine
  • Turmeric added carefully to warm drink patterns when the flavor fits

How to use this guide

Choose one or two beverage habits you can keep simple. A stable drink routine usually matters more than chasing constant novelty.
