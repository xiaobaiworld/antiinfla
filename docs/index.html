Site principles

Professional health content relies on tone, hierarchy, and restraint.

Readable hierarchy

Every page should work for both scanning and deep reading, with clear summaries, subheads, and related links.

Warm but not vague

The visual language should feel calm and premium without becoming overly soft, decorative, or trend-driven.

SEO by structure

Separate URLs, unique metadata, topic-focused pages, and internal linking matter more than clever wording alone.

Core topics

What readers usually want to know before they trust a wellness site.

Most readers are not searching for one giant explanation. They are searching for meal patterns, practical food examples, and easier starting points. A serious content site should meet that behavior directly.

That is why the site now focuses on a small cluster of pages rather than a single decorative homepage. This is the right direction for indexing, future article growth, and eventual product alignment.
