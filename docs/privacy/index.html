Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Anti-inflammatory foods may collect, how that information is used, and the practical limits of a simple content website.

Effective date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of March 23, 2026.

What information this site collects

Anti-inflammatory foods is a content website. The site does not provide account registration, paid checkout, or user profile features.

The site may collect limited technical information through standard hosting or server logs, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate location, and pages visited.

How information is used

  • To keep the site running, secure, and available

  • To detect spam, abuse, or technical issues

Analytics and cookies

The current public site may use a privacy-aware analytics script to understand overall page usage, popular content, and broad traffic patterns. Basic technical information may also appear in normal hosting or server logs used to keep the site available and secure.

Analytics tools may set or rely on standard cookies or similar measurement technology. The site does not use analytics to create personal user accounts or publish personally identifying activity histories.

Third-party links

Some pages link to outside resources, such as nutrition references or public health organizations. Anti-inflammatory foods is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Data retention

Technical logs may be retained for as long as reasonably needed for security, operational analysis, and site maintenance, subject to the policies of the hosting providers in use.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date above.

Contact
