Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Anti-inflammatory foods may collect, how that information may be used, and what that usually means in practice for a simple content website.

Effective date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of April 21, 2026.

What information this site collects

Anti-inflammatory foods is a content website. The site does not provide account registration, paid checkout, or user profile features.

The site may collect limited technical information through standard hosting or server logs, such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate location, and pages visited.

How information is used

  • To keep the site running, secure, and available
  • To understand which pages are being used and improve site content
  • To detect spam, abuse, or technical issues

Analytics and cookies

The current public site may use a privacy-aware analytics script to understand overall page usage, popular content, and broad traffic patterns. Basic technical information may also appear in normal hosting or server logs used to keep the site available and secure.

Analytics tools may set or rely on standard cookies or similar measurement technology. The site does not use analytics to create personal user accounts or publish personally identifying activity histories.

Advertising and third-party vendors

The site may use advertising services, including Google AdSense or similar third-party vendors, to show ads in some parts of the site. If that happens, those vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to help measure ad performance and make advertising more relevant.

Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites. If you want to understand that process in more detail, you can visit Google's partner sites policy page. If you would rather manage ad personalization settings directly, you can do that through Google Ads Settings.

Third-party links

Some pages link to outside resources, such as nutrition references or public health organizations. Those sites have their own privacy practices and content policies, so it is a good idea to review them directly if you choose to visit.

Data retention

Technical logs may be retained for as long as reasonably needed for security, operational analysis, and site maintenance, subject to the policies of the hosting providers in use.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date above.

Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, contact the site operator at admin@antiinflammatorydiets.com.
