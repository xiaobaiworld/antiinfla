Contact

If you want to ask a site question, report an error, or reach out about privacy or content, this is the easiest place to do it.

Email

You can contact the site operator at admin@antiinflammatorydiets.com.

Good reasons to reach out

  • You found a factual error, broken link, or confusing page
  • You have a privacy question about the site
  • You want to point out a food or guide topic that would be useful to cover
  • You have a business, partnership, or site-related inquiry

Helpful details to include

If you are reporting a correction, include the page URL, the sentence or section that seems wrong, and a short note about what should be checked. If you are suggesting a topic, explain what question you hoped the site would answer and why it would be useful for readers.

Editorial and correction notes

Content feedback is reviewed with the site's editorial policy in mind. The goal is to keep pages practical, careful, and useful without overstating what any single food can do.

Before you send a message

This site shares general educational information and does not provide personal medical advice. If your question is about symptoms, treatment, medications, allergies, or a personal medical condition, a qualified healthcare professional will be able to give you more personal guidance.

Response expectations

Messages are read as time allows. Site feedback, correction notes, and privacy questions are usually the easiest to respond to clearly and usefully.
